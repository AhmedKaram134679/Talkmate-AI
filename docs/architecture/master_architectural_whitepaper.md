## 1. Executive Summary

TalkMate AI represents a paradigm shift in real-time communication, introducing
**Sovereign Intelligence**. This architecture ensures that AI is not just a
feature, but a governed, accountable, and privacy-first entity integrated into
the core media fabric.

## 2. Layered Governance Framework (D1 → F8)

The architecture is built on three foundational pillars:

### I. The Observability & Stability Foundation (D1-D2)

- **Predictive Health**: Real-time forecasting of system failures.
- **Circuit Breakers**: Immediate isolation of unstable external services.

### II. The Intelligence & Adaptive Brain (D3, D6, F1-F3)

- **Adaptive Controller**: Dynamic resource back-off based on hardware feedback.
- **Intelligence Engine**: Real-time signal analysis for echo, noise, and
  jitter.

### III. The Sovereignty & Compliance Shield (D4-D5, E-F)

- **Feature Gatekeeper**: Constitutional control over resource access.
- **Silent AI Co-Moderator**: Advisor-only AI within a Human-in-the-Loop
  protocol.
- **Institutional Accountability**: Tamper-proof logging of every sovereign
  decision.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: AES-256 encrypted legal evidence packs (GDPR/AI Act
  ready).

- **Zero-Content Analysis**: All AI analysis is performed on metadata; no user
  media is recorded or processed.
- **Atomic Purge Protocol**: Immediate and complete destruction of session
  metadata across all layers upon termination.
- **Explainable Compliance**: Technical decisions are translated into
